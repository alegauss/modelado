Modelado example files

The examples on Modelado's start screen are the inputs its site demonstrates.

chair.png, burger.png
  From TripoSR's examples:
  https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/TripoSR/tree/107cefdc244c39106fa830359024f6a2f1c78871/examples
  Licensed under the MIT licence below.

chair.glb
  The model Modelado made from chair.png (modelado-cli object --resolution 320, then
  edit --simplify 0.35).

  Marble bust of Herodotus, The Metropolitan Museum of Art, CC0.

cathedral.jpg

  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Statue_of_Saint_Paul_by_Ignazio_Marabitti_and_Syracuse_Cathedral%27s_Baroque_Facade.jpg
